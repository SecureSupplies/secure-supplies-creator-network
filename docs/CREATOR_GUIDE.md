# CREATOR_GUIDE

Welcome to the Creator Command Center. Learn. Build. Publish. Refer. Earn.

## Getting started
1. **Apply** at /apply — free; no purchase ever required.
2. **Complete the Academy** — disclosure rules, approved/prohibited claims, and how commissions work. Training unlocks campaigns.
3. **Pick a campaign** in the Marketplace. Each card shows commission, content-reward rate, remaining budget, languages, platforms, and difficulty.
4. **Create** in the Remix Studio (trim, reframe 9:16/1:1/16:9, subtitles, hooks, logo, disclosure overlay, CTA + your promo code) or produce original content.
5. **Publish** with the required disclosure visible IN the content: "Paid affiliate link — I may earn a commission if you purchase or join through this link." For video: show it early, say it out loud, repeat it in the caption.
6. **Submit** the live URL + analytics evidence in the Share Center.
7. **Track** clicks, conversions, commissions, rewards, and payouts on your dashboard.

## How you earn
- Retail commissions on verified sales (25% first month / 15% renewals / 20% annual / 25% products, up to 30% launches) — calculated on Net Collected Revenue after fees, refunds, and taxes.
- Content rewards for approved posts ($5–$20 bonus + per-1,000-view rewards, capped per post).
- Earnings are NOT guaranteed. Nobody is paid for recruiting. Self-referrals are never paid.

## Payouts
$50 minimum · monthly batches · 30–45 day hold (up to 90 days iOS) · refunds reverse commissions · negative balances carry forward.

## Never do this (see /policies/marketing)
Income promises · fabricated screenshots · purchased views/bots · spam · impersonation · unlicensed clips · "free energy," guaranteed fuel-savings, emissions, or certification claims · selling access outside authorized channels · sharing paid files.
